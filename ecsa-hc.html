Why this summit

Tanzania has made strong progress in digital health, yet interoperability, workforce capacity and sustainable scaling still limit its full potential. Many young health professionals have little structured exposure to digital health, AI, entrepreneurship and health systems innovation. The summit builds those skills and connects emerging innovators with health leaders and partners. It supports SDG 3 and SDG 9.

What happens at the summit

  • Capacity building sessionsPractical training in design thinking, digital health, AI, research and entrepreneurship.
  • Youth panel discussionsExpert dialogue on the future of healthcare innovation in Tanzania.
  • Innovation challenge competitionTen innovations will be pitched to solve real healthcare challenges, with mentorship.
  • Quiz challengeA test of knowledge in digital health, public health, health systems and emerging technologies.
  • Networking and mentorshipConnections with health leaders, innovators, development partners and mentors.
